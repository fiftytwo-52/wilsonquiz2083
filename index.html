Senior Quiz Rules

General

8 rounds: 5 general rounds (Biology, Physics, Chemistry, Geology & Astronomy, Miscellaneous), plus Audio Visual, Rapid Fire, and Gambling. General rounds have 8 questions each. Each question carries 10 marks; a passed question carries 5 marks. No negative marking, except in Audio Visual and Gambling.

Answering time

30 seconds, or 15 seconds for a passed question.

Audio Visual

Buzzer round with no passing. A wrong answer receives −5 marks.

Rapid Fire

Each team gets 10 questions in 1 minute. No negative marking.

Gambling

Gold = 5 marks, Diamond = 10 marks, Platinum = 15 marks. Wrong answers receive equal negative marks. Teams may skip a question. Trailing teams pick difficulty first.
